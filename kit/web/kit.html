Desk Kit

Make a security desk for your project: your policy, your advisories and when they apply, and an assistant that users and other agentic websites can ask. No server, and you pay for no model calls.

Build your desk

Paste your desk.json and the address where you will host it. The kit checks it, builds the agent network with the runtime's own parser and validator, and gives you a zip to unzip on any static host. Nothing leaves this page.

desk.json format

  • project, ecosystem (npm or PyPI), network (such as mypkg_security_desk), tagline, maintainers, theme (a dark color).
  • report: your private security contact, days to acknowledge, days to disclosure, what to include.
  • supported: version lines such as 3.x or 1.7.x and older, each with a status and a note.
  • advisories: OSV records (schema 1.6). Add database_specific.desk with affectedWhen, notAffectedWhen, workarounds, fixedIn and an optional vexHint. This is the knowledge scanners do not have.
  • sampleQueries: four to six questions your desk can answer, and one it must decline.

The desk publishes your advisories as an OSV feed too (osv/index.json), so tools can read them without the assistant. The CLI does the same: node kit/build_desk.mjs desk.json --out site --base-url https://…/.
